The problem

Every tool remembers.
None of it reaches past the tool it lives in.

What last month's job taught you sits in a folder this month's tool cannot see. So you file the context and point the agents at it, on every job, all day.

The walls

A dozen tools, each with its own corner. A tool remembers the chat, never the timeline, the approved take, or the decision that stuck.

You are the connector

The producer is the only thing carrying context between tools. That time comes straight out of the hours you would rather spend making the work.

Rented memory

What memory does exist sits on someone else's servers, priced by the seat, and stays there when you leave.

What it does

Three things.
Nothing you have to run.

It remembers for every tool

Claude, Codex, Cursor, your editors' laptops, your notes, your meetings. Every tool plugs into the same memory and asks it before answering.

It checks itself every night

Stale notes, duplicates, and two people's notes that disagree get flagged for a human. Nothing is ever deleted.

Nothing leaves your building

It runs on its own local models, on a private, encrypted network, behind a door only your approved machines can open.

It does not touch your project files or replace your editing tools. It remembers what your team decided and learned, from every seat.

Integrations

Connects to the tools you already use.
Three included.

An integration is a connector that brings what is in one of your tools into the machine, on a schedule. You pick the tool inside each category.

CategoryExamples
Your AI toolClaude Code, Codex, Cursor, claude.ai. This one is two-way: your agents ask the machine and write to it.
Documents and notesNotion, Google Docs, Coda, Craft, Evernote, Apple Notes. A folder of plain files needs no integration.
FilesGoogle Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, SharePoint, your office file server
ChatSlack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Google Chat
EmailGmail, Microsoft 365
Project trackerClickUp, Asana, Trello, Monday, Linear, Jira, Notion boards
MeetingsZoom or Meet recordings, Fireflies, Otter, a folder of recordings

If a tool has an export or an API, it is on the menu. If it only has a screen, it is not.
